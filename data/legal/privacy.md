# CONQUEER — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 16 October 2026
**Data controller:** KWIWI DIGITAL SOLUTIONS, based in Kenya ("we", "us", "CONQUEER")
**Contact for privacy matters:** @conqueeradmin on Telegram

This policy explains what personal data the CONQUEER Telegram bot collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. By using CONQUEER you agree to it.

---

## 1. The short version

- You must be **18 or older.**
- We collect only what the bot needs to work: your Telegram details, the profile you build, your photos, your approximate location, and your activity (likes, matches, reports).
- Other users see your profile, but only the parts you chose to share. **We never sell your data.**
- Some of what you share is **sensitive** (it can reveal your sexual orientation). We ask for your consent and treat it carefully.
- You can ask us to delete everything. We do it within **30 days.**

## 2. What we collect

**Provided by Telegram when you start the bot**
- Your Telegram user ID, username (if you have one), and language setting.

**What you give us when creating a profile**
- Name, age, role (Top / Bottom / Verse), hosting preference, city, a short description.
- Photos (up to 3) and your photo privacy setting.
- Your location: either the city you type, or **coordinates if you choose to share your location**. We use this to show distance to other users.

**What we record when you use the bot**
- Your selected mode (Fun / Dating / Friends) and search filters (age range, hosting, role).
- Profiles you like or pass on, matches, and any short message you send with a like.
- Which photos you have revealed and when (so "one reveal" rules work).
- Reports you make or receive, and moderation actions (warnings, bans).
- Referral information if you joined through an invite link.
- Technical logs (for example, actions and errors) used to run and secure the service.

We do **not** ask for your real legal name, ID documents, phone number or email.

## 3. Why we use your data

| Purpose | Data used |
|---|---|
| Create and show your profile to other users | Profile details, photos |
| Match you with compatible people | Age, role, mode, hosting, distance, filters |
| Blur and reveal photos according to your settings | Photos, privacy setting, reveal records |
| Notify you about likes and matches | Telegram ID, activity |
| Keep the community safe (reports, bans, removing underage users) | Profile, reports, logs |
| Run, secure and improve the service | Logs, aggregate statistics |
| Send you announcements about CONQUEER | Telegram ID |

We rely on **your consent** and on our legitimate interest in operating a safe service.

## 4. Sensitive data

Using a platform for LGBTQ+ connection can reveal information about your sexual orientation or sex life, which is **sensitive personal data**. By creating a profile you consent to us processing it for the purposes above. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (Section 9), though that means you can no longer use CONQUEER.

## 5. Who can see your data

**Other users** can see what you put on your profile: name, age, city (or distance if both users share location), description, role, hosting preference and photos (subject to your blur/reveal setting). Do not put anything on your profile you are not comfortable with other users seeing.

**Moderators and us.** We, and any moderators we appoint, can access profiles and reports when reviewing them. The operator also sees account sign-ups and basic activity statistics.

**Service providers.**
- **Telegram** carries all messages and photos. Telegram's own terms and privacy policy apply to that data.
- **Our hosting provider (Railway)** stores our database and runs the bot. Servers may be located outside Kenya, for example in Europe or the United States.

**Legal requests.** We may disclose information if we are legally required to, for example by a valid court order, or where necessary to protect someone from serious harm. We will take reasonable steps to review such requests before responding.

**We do not sell** your personal data or share it with advertisers.

## 6. Photos and screenshots

We offer blur-and-reveal and use Telegram's content protection on photos we send. These features reduce copying and forwarding but **cannot fully prevent** screenshots, screen recordings or photos of a screen. Do not upload photos you would be uncomfortable with someone capturing.

## 7. How long we keep your data

- **Active accounts:** while you use CONQUEER.
- **Disabled profiles:** your profile is hidden but your data remains until you ask us to delete it.
- **After a deletion request:** we delete your profile, photos, likes, matches and related records within **30 days**, except for anything we are legally required to keep.
- **Logs and backups** may persist for a short additional period before being overwritten.
- If we are investigating a report or violation involving your account, we may delay deletion until that is resolved.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access controls, a protected database, and limiting what moderators can see. No online service is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that your data can never be accessed without authorisation. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the Kenyan data protection authority as required by law.

## 9. Your rights

Under the **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019** (and similar laws where they apply), you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate data (you can also edit your profile in the bot).
- **Delete** your data.
- **Object** to or restrict certain processing.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time.
- Request a **copy** of your data in a common format.

To use these rights, message **@conqueeradmin** on Telegram. We may need to verify that the request comes from your Telegram account. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the period the law requires.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya).**

## 10. International transfers

Because our hosting provider and Telegram operate globally, your data may be processed outside Kenya. We choose providers that apply reasonable safeguards, and by using CONQUEER you agree to this.

## 11. Children

CONQUEER is **strictly for adults (18+).** We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using the bot, report the profile or contact us and we will remove it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will tell you about important changes through the bot. Continuing to use CONQUEER after an update means you accept it.

## 13. Contact

Privacy questions, requests or complaints: **@conqueeradmin** on Telegram.
